# Privacy policy

**Instant Swap for Google Photos** collects no data.

Last updated: 15 September 2026.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. The extension has no server, no account, and no analytics. It sends no network request of its own.

## What the extension stores

Only your own settings: whether the swap is on, what to shorten, and two advanced switches. Chrome keeps them in its
`storage.sync` area, so they follow your Chrome profile. The extension never reads or stores anything about your photos,
your albums, or your Google account.

## Why the extension needs its permissions

| Permission                  | Why                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `storage`                   | To save your settings, so the options page keeps its values.                         |
| `https://photos.google.com` | To shorten the slide animation on that site. The extension runs on no other website. |

## What the extension does on the page

It shortens animation timing, and nothing else. It reads no page content, clicks no button, and changes no photo.
