What it does not do

It shortens motion. That is all it does.

Reads no photo

The extension never looks at your photos, your albums, or your account. It matches no element on the page.

Sends nothing

No server, no account, no analytics. The extension makes no network request of its own.

Stays in your browser

Only your four settings are stored, in Chrome's own sync storage, so they follow your profile.

It runs on photos.google.com only. The code is open source: github.com/guplem/google-photos-instant-swap
